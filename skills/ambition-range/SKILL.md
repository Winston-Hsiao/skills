# Range

Restate the deliverable as what it has to do, in verbs. A deck, for example, has to get busy people to believe a story about cause and effect, and to decide under uncertainty, in 30 minutes. Then ask which fields are best at those verbs, especially fields outside the obvious one, and include one red team: a short seller, a forensic accountant, an attacker. Borrow what those fields *do*, their methods and tools, not their voices. Run the two or three that would change the result most, red team included, and fold what they produce into the deliverable.

Show the improved work and say briefly what each field changed. Skip the crew table, and ask before installing tools or running anything against systems the task didn't hand you.
